AVAILABLE NOW · DATA STUDIO

Clean up CSV and Excel files in seconds.

Upload a CSV or XLSX workbook to inspect its structure, preview records and identify blank or duplicate rows. Choose cleanup and transformation options, then download a cleaned or reshaped CSV or XLSX workbook. Files are processed in memory and are not saved to disk.

AVAILABLE NOW · RESEARCH STUDIO

Turn source material into a useful brief.

Add a research question and up to eight sources. Paste the relevant notes or excerpts, then generate a structured brief with linked references, key points and review limitations. This version organizes supplied material; it does not browse or verify pages automatically.

AVAILABLE NOW · DOCUMENT STUDIO

Extract text and metadata from documents.

Upload a PDF, Word DOCX or UTF-8 TXT file. Extracted text and available metadata are shown immediately and can be downloaded as a plain-text file. Documents are processed in memory, not saved to disk.
